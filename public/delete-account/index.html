Your account

Delete your account

You can delete your nexGen AI Chat account from any device using the app.

  1. Open nexGen AI Chat and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open the account menu in the left sidebar and select Delete account.
  3. Confirm the prompt to permanently delete the account.

What deletion removes: the account, active sessions, conversations, and messages associated with it in the application database. Deletion cannot be undone. Infrastructure logs or backups may persist temporarily under service-provider retention practices.

Before you delete

Download any conversation PDF you want to keep before confirming. The PDF export is created in your browser.
