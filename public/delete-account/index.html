Account deletion instructions

You can permanently delete your nexGen account and the data associated with it from inside the app.

  1. Open nexGen and sign in.
  2. Open the account menu in the left navigation.
  3. Select Delete account and confirm the deletion.

Deletion removes your account, active sessions, conversations, and messages stored by nexGen. The action cannot be undone. Backups or provider-side logs may be retained for a limited period where required for security, legal, or operational reasons.

If you cannot sign in, email tsekpochris101@gmail.com from the address associated with the account and request deletion.
