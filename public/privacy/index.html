Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

nexGen is an AI chat assistant for conversations, answers, writing, brainstorming, learning, and productivity. This policy explains what the service stores and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Account details such as your name and email address when you create an account.
  • A securely hashed password and a session cookie used to keep you signed in. We do not store your plain-text password.
  • Conversations and messages that you create in the service.
  • If you turn on Private personalization in Settings, short response-style preferences (for example, preferred answer length or formatting) inferred from recent user messages or entered by you.
  • Technical information needed to operate the service, such as request and error logs from the hosting provider.

How information is used

We use this information to authenticate you, save and display your conversations, respond to requests, protect the service, and improve reliability. When you request an AI response, the relevant conversation history is sent to the configured AI provider to generate that response. If you enable Private personalization in Settings, recent user messages may also be sent to the configured AI provider to infer response-style preferences. Only the resulting short preferences are saved in a profile for your account and used to tailor later responses; raw messages remain in chat history and are not copied into the preference profile. Personalization is off by default. You can review, edit, clear, or disable it in Settings. It does not train a shared model. If you enable web search, the requested query is sent to the configured search provider.

Storage and retention

Account, conversation, and (when enabled) preference data may be stored in the service database. We retain it while your account is active or as needed to provide the service. You can clear personalization preferences from Settings or delete your account from the signed-in account menu; account deletion removes the account, sessions, conversations, messages, and associated preference data stored by nexGen.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with service providers needed to host the app, store data, generate AI responses, or perform an explicitly requested web search. Providers process information according to their own policies.

Children

nexGen is not directed to children under 13. Do not create an account or submit personal information if you are under the minimum age permitted in your location.
