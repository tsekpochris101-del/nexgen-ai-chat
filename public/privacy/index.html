Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

This notice describes how nexGen AI Chat handles information when you use the service at nexgen-ai-chat-ctkdev.vercel.app.

Information the service handles

  • Account details: your name, email address, and a password hash used to authenticate your account. The service does not store your password in readable form.
  • Conversation content: messages you send, text from supported attachments, AI replies, conversation titles, and related timestamps.
  • Session and service data: an authentication cookie and technical request information used to operate, secure, and diagnose the service. Vercel Web Analytics may collect site usage metrics.

How information is used

Account and conversation data are used to sign you in, save your chat history, and provide the features you request. Chat prompts and relevant conversation context are sent to Groq to generate replies. When you use web search, search queries are sent to Exa. Image-search queries are sent to Wikimedia Commons. When you request an AI image, the image prompt is sent to Pollinations. Those providers process requests under their own terms and privacy practices.

Conversation records are stored in the service's PostgreSQL database hosted by Neon. Vercel hosts the website and its API and may process technical data needed to deliver and secure the service.

Cookies, downloads, and choices

The service uses a first-party session cookie to keep you signed in. It is marked HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax, and is Secure in production. You can sign out to end the session. The PDF export is created in your browser and downloaded to your device.

You can choose whether to use web search, image search, and image generation. Do not include sensitive information in prompts or attachments unless you are comfortable with the relevant provider processing it.

Retention and deletion

Your account and conversation records remain in the service until you delete your account. Use the Delete account action in the app and confirm the request. A successful deletion removes the account, its sessions, conversations, and messages from the application database. Service providers may retain operational logs or backups for limited periods under their own retention practices.

Account deletion is permanent. Download any conversation PDF you want to keep before confirming deletion.

Security and changes

The service uses password hashing and a protected session cookie. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. This policy may be updated when the service or its data practices change; the effective date above will be updated with material revisions.
